The law on repairs

What your landlord must fix.

The Landlord and Tenant Act 1985 makes your landlord responsible for the structure and exterior of your home, and for its water, gas, electricity, heating and sanitation. Since 2019 the law also says your home must be fit to live in for the whole of your tenancy.

  • Damp and mould

    Black mould, condensation or rising damp

  • Heating or hot water

    A boiler, radiators or hot water that do not work

  • Leaks and water damage

    Burst pipes, or water through the roof or ceiling

  • Electrical faults

    Unsafe wiring or sockets, or loss of power

  • Structural problems

    Cracks, subsidence, or unsafe walls or ceilings

  • Pests

    Mice, rats, cockroaches or similar

  • Plumbing and drainage

    Blocked drains, toilets or taps

  • Unsafe conditions

    Any other serious repair still outstanding

Awaab's Law

Your landlord is on the clock.

Awaab's Law: since 27 October 2025, council and housing association landlords in England must act on damp, mould and dangers within set times. If the home cannot be made safe in time, the landlord must offer somewhere suitable to stay, at its own cost.

to make an emergency hazard safe
10 working days
to investigate significant damp and mould
5 working days
to do the safety work after that

Questions

Housing disrepair, answered.

The questions tenants ask most.

What is housing disrepair?

Housing disrepair is when your landlord has not kept your rented home in repair or fit to live in. Under the Landlord and Tenant Act 1985, landlords must keep the structure and exterior in repair, along with the water, gas, electricity, heating and sanitation, and since 2019 the home must be fit to live in for the whole tenancy.

Can I claim compensation for housing disrepair?

You may be able to claim if you rent from the council or a housing association in England, you told your landlord about the problem, and it still has not been put right. A claim can lead to compensation and to a court ordering the repairs.

How much compensation could I get for housing disrepair?

There is no fixed amount. It depends on how bad the problem was, how long it lasted and how it affected you, including damaged belongings, extra costs and your health.

What is Awaab's Law?

Awaab's Law sets repair deadlines for council and housing association landlords in England, in force since 27 October 2025. They must make an emergency hazard safe within 24 hours, investigate significant damp and mould within 10 working days, and do the safety work within 5 working days after that.

How long do I have to make a housing disrepair claim?

Up to 6 years from when your landlord should have done the work, or 3 years if you are claiming for an injury to your health.
